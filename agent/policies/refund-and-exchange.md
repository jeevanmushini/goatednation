# RETURN, SIZING EXCHANGE & REFUND POLICY
**Window:** 7 Days from Date of Delivery | **Integrated Partner:** Shiprocket Express Returns  
**Support Desk:** https://goatednation.com/support  
**Statutory Basis:** Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 (Rule 4(11) & Rule 6); Sale of Goods Act, 1930  
**Frontend Route:** `/policies/refund-and-exchange`

---

### 1. Our Quality & Fit Guarantee
Streetwear silhouettes are boxy, heavyweight (280–420 GSM), and cut differently than commercial mass-market fashion. We want every piece you collect from GOATED NATION to fit exactly as intended. Therefore, we offer a **7-Day Sizing Exchange & Return Protocol** starting from the moment the courier marks your package as `DELIVERED`.

---

### 2. Eligibility Conditions for Returns & Exchanges
To qualify for an exchange or return, garments must meet the following mandatory criteria:
1. **Pristine Condition:** The garment must be completely unworn, unwashed, and free of any perfume, deodorant, smoke, or detergent odors.
2. **Tags & Packaging Intact:** All original hangtags, barcode tags, and branded custom frosted polybags must remain attached and undamaged.
3. **Window Compliance:** The exchange or return request must be logged via our Support Portal (`/support`) within **7 calendar days** of verified delivery.
4. **Non-Returnable Items:** Due to hygiene standards, headwear, socks, and items marked explicitly as "Final Archive Clearance" cannot be returned unless manufacturing distress is proven.

---

### 3. Sizing Exchange Workflow (100% Automated via Shiprocket)
1. **Log Request:** Navigate to `/support` and select category **"Sizing & Fit Exchange"**.
2. **Select Replacement:** Select your order number and desired replacement size (`S`, `M`, `L`, `XL`, `XXL`).
3. **Reverse Pickup Scheduled:** Upon support approval, our system schedules a reverse courier pickup from your delivery address via Shiprocket (Delhivery / Blue Dart Express Return).
4. **Handover:** Place the folded garment in its original bag. The courier agent will arrive to collect the package with a digital reverse AWB code.
5. **Dispatch of Replacement:** Once the courier scans the package at our Bangalore logistics hub, your replacement size is manifested and dispatched with priority tracking.

---

### 4. Garment Defect or Wrong Item Protocol
If your package arrives damaged in transit, or displays print or stitching distress:
1. Open a ticket at `/support` under **"Garment Defect or Transit Damage"** within **48 hours** of unboxing.
2. Upload 1–2 clear photographs displaying the defect alongside the original tag.
3. Upon review, we will dispatch an immediate replacement or issue a **100% refund**, with zero return shipping deductions.

---

### 5. Refund Processing Timelines & Bank Settlement
In accordance with Rule 4(11) of the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020:
- **Refund Initiation:** Refunds are authorized immediately upon return inspection approval (within **24 hours**).
- **Payment Gateway Execution:** Funds are released electronically via **Razorpay** to your original payment method.
- **Credit Settlement Window:**
  - **UPI Transactions:** 24 to 48 hours.
  - **NetBanking / Debit Cards:** 3 to 5 business days.
  - **Credit Cards:** 5 to 7 business days (depending on your issuing bank's billing cycle).
- **Credit Note Receipt:** You will receive a formal digital **Refund Confirmation Note** via email containing your official Razorpay Refund ID (`rfnd_XXXXX`) for banking verification.
