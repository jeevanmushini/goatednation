### 1. Geographic Coverage & Serviceability
GOATED NATION delivers to **over 27,000+ PIN codes across all 28 States and 8 Union Territories** of India through our integrated courier network:
- **Primary Courier Partners:** Delhivery Surface & Air, Blue Dart Express, DTDC, Xpressbees, Shadowfax.
- **Pincode Verification:** You can check serviceability and estimated delivery times live on the product deck before placing an order.

---

### 2. Processing & Dispatch Timelines
- **Order Manifestation:** All orders received before 2:00 PM IST on business days are processed, packed, and manifested the same day.
- **Dispatch SLA:** Orders are handed over to couriers within **24 to 48 business hours** of payment confirmation.
- **Drop Capsules:** During high-volume limited capsule releases, dispatch may take up to 72 hours due to heavy artisan packaging standards.
- **Sundays & Public Holidays:** Operations and courier pickups are paused on Sundays and national holidays.

### 4. Real-Time Tracking & Notifications
- The moment your parcel is manifested, a live Air Waybill (AWB) number is generated.
- You will receive tracking milestone notifications via email at every stage:
  1. `SHIPMENT BOOKED` (AWB Assigned)
  2. `IN TRANSIT` (Movement across regional logistics hubs)
  3. `OUT FOR DELIVERY` (Assigned to local delivery associate)
  4. `DELIVERED` (Receipt confirmation)
- You can track your shipment anytime by entering your Order ID or AWB at `https://goatednation.com/track`.

---

### 5. Delivery Attempts & Non-Delivery Report (NDR) Protocol
- Couriers make up to **3 consecutive delivery attempts**.
- If you are unavailable, the courier logs an **NDR (Non-Delivery Report)**.
- Our automated logistics system alerts you via email/SMS allowing you to:
  - Reschedule delivery to a preferred date.
  - Correct an incomplete phone number or street landmark.
  - Or request self-pickup from the local courier delivery hub.
- If a parcel remains undelivered after 3 attempts, it is marked as `RTO` (Return to Origin). Our support desk will contact you to arrange free re-dispatch.
