### 1. Introduction & Scope
GOATED NATION is committed to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and security of our customers ("Data Principals"). This Privacy Notice is issued in compliance with the provisions of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), the Information Technology Act, 2000, and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011.

This notice explains how we collect, process, store, transfer, and protect your personal data when you visit our website (goatednation.com), purchase our streetwear garments, or communicate with our support desk.

---

### 2. Categories of Personal Data Collected
We collect only the minimum necessary personal data required to fulfill our e-commerce obligations ("Data Minimization"):

1. **Identity & Contact Data:** Full Legal Name, Email Address, Delivery Phone Number.
2. **Delivery & Logistics Data:** Physical Postal Address, Landmark, City, State, PIN Code (shared with our logistics partner, Shiprocket, solely for order delivery).
3. **Transaction & Payment Metadata:** Razorpay Order ID, Razorpay Payment ID, payment method used (UPI, NetBanking, Credit/Debit Card).  
   *Note: GOATED NATION never collects, processes, or stores your 16-digit debit/credit card numbers, CVVs, or NetBanking passwords. All payment transactions are encrypted and processed by RBI-licensed Payment Aggregators (Razorpay) under Card-on-File Tokenization (CoFT) guidelines.*
4. **Technical & Telemetry Data:** IP address, browser type, device operating system, access timestamps, and session cookies (utilized for cart persistence and fraud prevention).
5. **Customer Support Data:** Photographs of garment sizing distress or manufacturing defects voluntarily submitted during ticket resolution, conversation transcripts, and customer satisfaction ratings.

---

### 3. Lawful Purpose of Data Processing
Under Section 4 and Section 6 of the DPDP Act 2023, your personal data is processed solely for lawful purposes for which you have provided clear, affirmative, and unambiguous consent:
- **Order Fulfillment & Logistics:** Processing orders, manifesting shipments with couriers, and generating tax invoices.
- **Account Administration:** Maintaining your member archive, order history, and wishlist in `/settings`.
- **Communications:** Dispatches of transactional receipts, OTP verification codes, logistics tracking updates, and critical security notices via Resend.
- **Support & Returns:** Processing reverse-pickup courier orders, sizing exchanges, and Razorpay refunds.
- **Legal & Statutory Compliance:** Adherence to the Central GST Act, 2017, and Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020.

---

### 4. Third-Party Data Disclosures & Sub-Processors
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third-party marketing brokers. Data is transferred strictly on a "need-to-know" basis to verified service partners under strict confidentiality agreements:
- **Logistics Aggregator:** Shiprocket (BigFoot Retail Solutions Pvt. Ltd.) and integrated courier partners (Delhivery, Blue Dart, DTDC) for package transit and live tracking.
- **Payment Gateway:** Razorpay Software Pvt. Ltd. (PCI-DSS Level 1 certified) for processing electronic payments.
- **Cloud Infrastructure & Persistence:** Neon PostgreSQL Serverless (AWS us-east-2 / Aiven Managed Services) for secure database storage with encryption at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3).
- **Asset Delivery & CDN:** Cloudinary Inc. for optimized garment imagery and secure defect proof uploads.
- **Transactional Email Gateway:** Resend Inc. for cryptographic OTPs and delivery notifications.

---

### 5. Rights of the Data Principal (Under DPDP Act 2023)
As a Data Principal residing in India, you are entitled to the following statutory rights:
1. **Right to Access Information:** You may review your personal information, past orders, and profile records at any time by logging into `/settings`.
2. **Right to Correction & Erasure:** You may request correction of inaccurate data or complete erasure of your account by contacting our Grievance Officer. Data required for statutory GST invoice audits (7 years) will be archived in compliance with Indian tax laws.
3. **Right to Grievance Redressal:** You have the right to register complaints regarding your data processing with our designated Grievance Officer, with escalation rights to the **Data Protection Board of India (DPBI)**.
4. **Right to Nominate:** In the event of death or incapacity, you have the right to nominate another individual to exercise your data rights.

---

### 6. Data Security & Storage
We implement industry-standard technical and organizational security measures:
- PBKDF2 with SHA-512 cryptographic salting for user credentials.
- HTTPS encryption across all endpoints using TLS 1.3.
- Secure, HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax session cookies preventing Cross-Site Scripting (XSS).
- Edge-level Content Security Policy (CSP) and Origin verification.
